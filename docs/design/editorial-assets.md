# Visual direction: Young Unicorn

This iteration introduces results before category imagery: buyers, orders, historical portfolio size, and year-on-year turnover growth. Source: the company welcome presentation. Absolute revenue, employee count, product ratings, and sold-unit counts have not been invented.

The photographs below are illustrations of business directions, not photographs of the company's staff or claims to show existing branded products. They were created with the built-in ImageGen tool. The website labels this imagery as a visual concept. Generated originals remain in the Codex image library; optimized WebP copies are stored in this repository.

## Fashion

Final file: `public/brand/fashion-editorial.webp` (1536 × 1024).

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: editorial image for Young Unicorn, an e-commerce group developing fashion and self-care brands. Create a wide landscape, magazine-quality fashion campaign photograph, aspect ratio 3:2. A woman and a man, both clearly adults in their late twenties, wearing refined contemporary everyday clothing: woman in a clean white oversized cotton t-shirt and relaxed ivory trousers; man in a charcoal cotton t-shirt with loose stone-coloured trousers. Candid waist-up crop, both relaxed and looking naturally away from camera, no staged corporate smiles. Architectural daylight studio, pale warm grey textured wall, a curved soft lilac panel partially visible behind them. Natural skin detail, beautiful cotton weave, subtle shadow, warm daylight with crisp sophisticated fashion editorial lighting. Art direction restrained and modern, akin an independent fashion magazine. Subjects positioned towards centre and right, no text anywhere, no logos, no graphics, no frames, no watermark. Realistic photography, no glossy 3D shapes, no CGI appearance. This is an illustrative fashion-direction photograph, not a portrait of company employees.

## Self-care

Final file: `public/brand/selfcare-editorial.webp` (1536 × 1024).

Final prompt:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset: self-care category editorial photograph for Young Unicorn e-commerce corporate website. Wide landscape aspect ratio 3:2. A beautifully restrained real studio still-life on warm cream limestone, a translucent frosted pale-lavender skincare bottle with matte white cap, a small satin white cream jar, and one sculptural folded ivory towel. A gentle strip of daylight and architectural shadows crosses the surface, pale lilac backdrop, subtle refractions through glass, real material texture and quiet luxury. Objects arranged off-centre towards the right and grounded physically on stone. Photograph with 80mm lens, real product photography, editorial rather than commercial stock, precise crafted composition. No legible text, no labels, no logos, no fake brand marks, no typography or decorative UI. No floating objects, no gradients painted into background, no plastic 3D rendering. This is illustrative self-care category imagery rather than a claim to show an existing commercial product.

## Direction video loops

`fashion-loop.mp4` and `selfcare-loop.mp4` are 12-second, silent H.264 loops at 1280 × 854, 24 fps. They are derived from the corresponding editorial photographs with a smooth, periodic camera crop, rather than newly filmed or AI-animated subject motion. Each clip is approximately 1 MB. The original photographs remain accessible fallbacks and print assets.

Playback is limited to visible cards and foreground tabs, with a shared pause control. Reduced-motion preferences keep the still images. No third-party media requests or new image generation are involved.

## Employee guide

No invented monthly onboarding milestones or automatic promotion dates are shown. The directory supports filtering by topic group.
