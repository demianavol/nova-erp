## Prompt

Create an 8-second photorealistic fashion editorial film for the homepage of Young Unicorn, featuring its women's clothing brand Enmoone. Landscape 16:9, one continuous shot, no cuts.

Use the attached clean product photograph as the exact garment reference. Preserve the white blouse's silhouette, fabric, collar, cuffs, buttons and waist details. An adult woman, approximately 28 years old, wears this blouse with tailored dark trousers. Natural appearance, restrained styling, relaxed confidence.

Place her in the RIGHT half of a refined architectural interior: warm taupe plaster, smoked oak and softly shadowed stone. Directional late-afternoon window light reveals the texture of the clothing. The palette is warm charcoal, espresso brown and ivory, with a very subtle muted lilac accent in the distant background. Rich midtones and soft highlights; natural skin and textile detail.

Keep the LEFT 45 percent of the frame calm, darker and visually simple, with softly textured architecture and no people or bright objects, reserved for a white website headline. Maintain this composition throughout the entire clip.

The camera makes an extremely slow, stabilized lateral glide with a slight push-in. The woman makes one subtle natural weight shift; a gentle breeze barely moves her hair and the blouse. Elegant, quiet, believable motion. Finish close to the opening composition and pose for a gentle repeating loop.

No text, logos, watermarks, prices, shopping interface, captions, speech or music. No cuts, fast movement, dramatic posing, garment changes, morphing, artificial skin, distorted anatomy, floating products, neon effects or glossy 3D graphics. Do not add accessories or change garment details from the reference.
